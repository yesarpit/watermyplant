The Best Indoor Plants for Indian Summers

TL;DR (Quick Answer): The best indoor plants to survive the harsh Indian summers are 1) Snake Plant (Sansevieria), 2) ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas), 3) Aloe Vera, 4) Rubber Plant, and 5) Spider Plant. These plants are drought-tolerant, require minimal watering, and thrive even when the AC is turned off.

Indian summers can be brutally hot, not just for us, but for our indoor greenery as well. When the temperature soars past 40°C and the AC isn't running 24/7, many delicate houseplants wilt and suffer.

If you're looking to add some green to your home without the stress of constant monitoring, here are the most resilient indoor plants for the Indian summer climate.

1. Snake Plant (Sansevieria)

Virtually indestructible, the Snake Plant thrives on neglect. It requires very little water and can survive in both bright indirect light and dark corners. It is also an excellent air purifier, releasing oxygen at night.

2. ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)

The ZZ plant has thick, waxy leaves that retain water perfectly, making it highly drought-resistant. It actually prefers dry soil, meaning you only need to water it once every few weeks even in the peak of summer.

3. Aloe Vera

A staple in many Indian households for its medicinal properties, Aloe Vera is a succulent that loves warmth and sunshine. Just place it near a sunny window and water it deeply but infrequently.

4. Rubber Plant (Ficus elastica)

The Rubber Plant, with its large, glossy leaves, can tolerate dry indoor air remarkably well. While it does need a bit more light than a ZZ plant, it holds up beautifully during the hot months provided it isn't overwatered.

Going Away This Summer?

Even the toughest plants need *some* water. If you are planning to travel to beat the heat this summer, don't let your plants dry out. Use a professional plant watering service to keep them hydrated.
