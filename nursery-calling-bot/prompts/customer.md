1. Greet the customer, say you're calling about their plant-watering enquiry, and check this is a good time.

2. Confirm they still need someone, and confirm the dates.
3. Confirm the exact area and one nearby landmark. Do not ask for the full house address. We share that
   only on WhatsApp once a gardener is confirmed.

5. Ask how often: daily, alternate days, or twice a week.
6. Ask how the gardener will get in: key with security, a neighbour, or someone at home.
7. Ask what budget per visit works for them. Don't quote or promise a price yourself.

- Never promise that a gardener is confirmed, and never promise a price.
