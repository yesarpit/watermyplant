Gardeners & nurseries: plant-watering work near you

Families across India go away for a few days and need someone to water their plants. We find those customers and match each one with a gardener near their home. If you or your team can take short jobs like this, tell us where you work.

  • ✅ You set your own rate per visit.
  • ✅ You hear the area, dates, visits and number of plants first, then say yes or no. Saying no is fine.
  • ✅ We only call gardeners who ask us to. That starts with your WhatsApp message below.

What the work is

Watering a customer's pots and balcony or indoor plants while they travel: usually daily or every other day, for a few days to a couple of weeks. Sometimes it's a little more: removing dry leaves or sending a photo on WhatsApp so the customer can see the plants are fine. We only share the customer's area before you agree. Once a job is confirmed you get the address and how to get in.

What you earn

You name your own price per visit. That price is yours: our fee goes on top of it, paid by the customer, and nothing is taken out of your rate. For example, if you quote ₹150 a visit, a 5-day daily job is 5 visits, or ₹750 for you. We don't charge you anything to join. If that ever changes, we'll ask you first. The work is occasional: it depends on who near you is travelling, so we can't promise how many jobs you'll get.

What happens next

  1. WhatsApp opens with your answers filled in. You press send, and that message is your request to be added.
  2. We reply on WhatsApp to confirm your number and area. We add you to our list only after that.
  3. When a customer near you needs help, we call you with the area, dates and visits. You say yes or no, and tell us your rate.

We use your number only for plant-care jobs, and we never share it with a customer before you agree to a job. Reply STOP at any time, and we'll take you off the list and stop calling you.
