RR Nagar (Rajarajeshwari Nagar), off Mysore Road in southwest Bangalore, is a mix of independent houses and apartment complexes. Most plant parents here keep a balcony or window-sill collection, and many houses also have a small front garden or terrace.

Bangalore's plant seasons, in short

  • March-May (dry, warm): Soil dries out fastest. Check pots in direct sun every 1-2 days; small terrace pots may need daily watering.
  • June-September (southwest monsoon): Rain does much of the work for outdoor pots. Water less and make sure pots drain. Waterlogged roots are a bigger risk than drought.
  • October-November (northeast monsoon): A second, often heavier rain spell. Same drainage caution, especially after several rainy days in a row.
  • December-February (cool, dry): Growth slows. Indoors, watering every 4-7 days is usually enough; overwatering now is a common cause of root rot.

Balcony and indoor plants in RR Nagar

For apartment balconies, the direction matters more than the season: a west-facing balcony gets harsh afternoon sun and needs checking every day in March-May, while a north- or east-facing one can often go 3-4 days. Indoor plants (ZZ, snake plant, pothos, peace lily) usually need water only once a week or less, and overwatering them is the most common way they die. If you have both, water the balcony plants on a schedule and check the indoor ones by feel.

Going away from RR Nagar?

For a trip of more than a few days, the seasonal shifts above can catch your plants out: a self-watering spike set for the dry season can drown a pot in monsoon rain, and a monsoon routine leaves pots thirsty in April.
