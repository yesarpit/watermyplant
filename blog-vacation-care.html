5 Ways to Keep Your Plants Alive While on Vacation

TL;DR (Quick Answer): To keep plants alive while on vacation: 1) Group them together to increase humidity, 2) Use self-watering spikes, 3) Create a pebble tray with water, 4) Keep them away from direct sunlight, and 5) For trips longer than a week, hire a professional plant watering service in India like watermyplant.in to ensure they survive and thrive.

Heading out for a much-needed vacation? While you pack your bags, you might be looking at your indoor jungle with a mix of love and anxiety. How will your calatheas survive without their daily misting? Will your fiddle leaf fig drop its leaves?

Here are the 5 best ways to ensure your green companions stay healthy while you are away.

1. Group Your Plants Together

Plants release moisture through a process called transpiration. By grouping your plants close together, you create a micro-environment with higher humidity, slowing down the rate at which the soil dries out. Keep them in a well-lit room but away from direct, harsh sunlight which evaporates water faster.

2. Use Self-Watering Spikes

For trips lasting 3 to 5 days, terracotta self-watering spikes or glass watering globes are excellent. They slowly release water into the soil as it dries. Just fill an old wine bottle or the globe with water, attach the spike, and push it deep into the pot.

3. Create a Humidity Tray

Fill a shallow tray with pebbles and pour water just below the top layer of the pebbles. Place your pots on top. As the water evaporates, it creates a humid environment right around the foliage. This is especially useful for ferns and calatheas.

4. The Bathtub Method

If you have a bathroom with a window, place your plants in the bathtub or shower on top of a damp towel. The bathroom naturally retains moisture, and the damp towel will slowly provide water from the bottom up. (Make sure your pots have drainage holes!).
