Plant Care in Kengeri, Bangalore (560060)

TL;DR (Quick Answer): Water more often in the March-May dry spell, cut back and watch drainage in the two monsoons (June-September and October-November), and water least in the cool December-February months. Kengeri, on Mysore Road in southwest Bangalore, has a lot of independent houses with terrace and front-yard pots alongside newer apartment blocks. Terrace plants here get more direct sun and wind than a shaded apartment balcony, so they dry out faster in the dry months and take the full force of the monsoon rain.

Bangalore's plant seasons, in short

  • March-May (dry, warm): Soil dries out fastest. Check pots in direct sun every 1-2 days; small terrace pots may need daily watering.
  • June-September (southwest monsoon): Rain does much of the work for outdoor pots. Water less and make sure pots drain. Waterlogged roots are a bigger risk than drought.
  • October-November (northeast monsoon): A second, often heavier rain spell. Same drainage caution, especially after several rainy days in a row.
  • December-February (cool, dry): Growth slows. Indoors, watering every 4-7 days is usually enough; overwatering now is a common cause of root rot.

Terrace and balcony pots in Kengeri

Open terraces are the trickiest spot. In March-May, small pots (under 8 inches) in full sun can dry out in a single day, so group them together, move them into afternoon shade if you can, and water early in the morning. In the monsoon, raise pots on bricks or stands so the drainage holes aren't sitting in pooled water. Money plants, snake plants, crotons, hibiscus and tulsi all do well on Kengeri terraces; tulsi and hibiscus are the ones that suffer first when watering is missed.

Going away from Kengeri?
